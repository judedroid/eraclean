Questions customers ask

Can I use Ero Clean for hand-washing clothes?

Yes. It is a multi-purpose liquid soap, so it can be used for laundry by hand as well as dishes, floors, hands and surfaces. Follow the instructions on the label.

Do you deliver to my area?

We deliver around Kampala, including Makerere, Nakawa, Ntinda, Kawempe, Rubaga and Kololo. See the delivery details on the Policies page.

How do I pay?

You can pay by MTN or Airtel mobile money, bank transfer (business customers), cash on delivery, or card through a licensed provider. See How to Pay.

What products do you sell?

Four product lines: multi-purpose liquid soap, bar soap, shower gel and shampoo. See all 13 sizes and packs on the Products page.

Do you sell to businesses?

Yes. Cafes, schools and offices can order 5 litre and 20 litre packs, ask for an invoice and arrange a monthly supply.

Is this a real shop?

No. Ero Clean is an invented business made by students for the BUC3106 E-Commerce capstone project at MUBS. No real orders or payments are taken.
