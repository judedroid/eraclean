About Ero Clean

Our story, our values and how our business works

Our story

Ero Clean began as a group project at Makerere University Business School. We noticed that many Kampala homes and small businesses buy a different soap for dishes, floors, laundry and hands. That costs more money and takes more space.

So we imagined one multi-purpose liquid soap that does all these jobs, sold in sizes for families and for cafes, schools and offices, and ordered easily on WhatsApp. We then added bar soap, shower gel and shampoo so one brand can look after the whole home.

Please note: Ero Clean is an invented business made by students for the BUC3106 E-Commerce capstone project. No real orders are taken.
