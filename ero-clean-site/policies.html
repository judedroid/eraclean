Policies

Delivery, returns, privacy, terms and how we keep things honest and safe

Delivery

  • We deliver in Kampala: Makerere, Nakawa, Ntinda, Kawempe, Rubaga and Kololo.
  • Orders confirmed before 2:00 pm are delivered the same day or the next working day.
  • Delivery fee: UGX 3,000 to 8,000 depending on distance SAMPLE. Free for orders above UGX 100,000 SAMPLE.
  • Check your order when it arrives, before you pay or sign.

Returns and refunds

  • Tell us within 24 hours if the product is damaged, leaking or not what you ordered. Send a photo on WhatsApp.
  • We replace the item or refund you to the same payment method.
  • We cannot take back opened bottles or used bars unless they are faulty.

Privacy notice

We follow Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019. This website does not collect any personal information: it has no forms, no accounts and no tracking. If Ero Clean were a real shop, we would handle your information like this:

QuestionAnswer
What we would collectYour name, phone number and delivery address.
WhyTo confirm your order, deliver it and contact you about it.
Who can see itOnly our team and our delivery rider. We never sell your information.
How long we keep itUp to 12 months, then we delete it.
Your rightsYou can ask to see, correct or delete your information. Call 0783 393 805 or 0703 316 223.

Terms

  • Prices are in Uganda shillings. The price we confirm on WhatsApp is the price you pay.
  • An order is final once payment is confirmed in our records.
  • We never ask for your password, mobile money PIN or card PIN.
  • Use each product as shown on its label. Keep away from children. Avoid contact with eyes and rinse with clean water if it happens. Do not drink liquid soap, gel or shampoo.
  • Ero Clean is an invented business for the BUC3106 E-Commerce capstone project at MUBS. No real orders or payments are taken. Anything marked SAMPLE is an example.

If someone pretends to be us or posts fake reviews

  1. Take screenshots and save the links.
  2. Report the page or post to the platform as impersonation or fake content.
  3. Warn our customers on our real pages and on WhatsApp, and tell them to pay only to the details on our How to Pay page.
  4. Ask the platform to remove fake reviews. We never write fake reviews ourselves.
  5. Real contact numbers: 0783 393 805 and 0703 316 223.

Website security

  • This site is served over https, so the padlock shows in the browser.
  • The site files hold no passwords or private information.
  • All our accounts use two-step verification and long passwords.
  • We never ask customers for a PIN or password.
