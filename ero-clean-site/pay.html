How to Pay and Order

Simple, safe ways to pay for your Ero Clean order

How to order

  1. Choose your product on the Products page and note its item code.
  2. Press Order on WhatsApp, or call 0783 393 805 or 0703 316 223. Send the item code, quantity and delivery area.
  3. We reply with the total and delivery time. Then you choose how to pay.

Pay to: Provider link sent on request SAMPLE (not active)

How we confirm a payment

We check our own mobile money, bank or payment provider records. We never accept a screenshot alone as proof of payment. We deliver only after the money shows in our records.

Refund rule

If your order arrives damaged, leaking or wrong, tell us on WhatsApp within 24 hours with a photo. We replace it or refund you to the same payment method.

Why we chose these methods

Mobile money (MTN and Airtel) is the way most Ugandan customers already pay, so it comes first. Cash on delivery helps customers who are not yet comfortable paying before they see the product. Bank transfer suits cafes, schools and offices that need an invoice. Card payment through a licensed provider is offered for customers who prefer it.

Cryptocurrency: we do not accept it. The Bank of Uganda has warned the public that cryptocurrencies are not legal tender in Uganda and are not regulated, so we stay with payment methods that are licensed and protect the customer.

Safety tips for customers

  • Only pay to the numbers and accounts shown on this page or sent from our WhatsApp numbers 0783 393 805 and 0703 316 223.
  • We will never ask for your PIN, password or card PIN.
  • If someone pretends to be Ero Clean, tell us right away.
